Most investors look at raw returns and call it a day. But a fund that made 20% last year might have simply got lucky — riding a sector that happened to boom. We dig deeper. We break down every fund manager's returns into four distinct sources, so you can see where the performance actually came from.

🏭

Sector Calls

Did the manager bet heavily on, say, banking or IT? Sometimes the whole sector rises — that's not skill, that's positioning. We measure how much of the return came from being in the right (or wrong) industries.

Style Bets

Growth vs value. Quality vs momentum. Every manager has a tilt. When growth stocks boom, growth-tilted funds look brilliant — but only compared to someone who tilted the other way. We strip this out.

Stock Picking

This is the holy grail. After accounting for sector and style, did the manager pick individual stocks that beat everything else in that category? This is true alpha — the rarest and most valuable skill.

Timing / Cash Calls

Did the manager sit on cash during a crash, or go all-in at the right time? Market timing is notoriously hard to repeat. We track how much of the return (or loss) came from these calls.

How We Score Funds

📈

Did it beat its index?

We compare each fund against its own benchmark index after fees. A fund earning its fees consistently is the baseline test.

40% of the score
🎯

How often did it win?

A fund beating its index in 7 out of 10 months is more trustworthy than one that had a single great year. Consistency matters far more than peaks.

35% of the score
🧭

Does it stick to its plan?

Managers who keep changing their approach are harder to trust. We reward funds that stay true to their stated strategy over time.

15% of the score
💸

Are the fees fair?

Annual fees eat into your returns every single year, compounding in reverse. We favour funds that charge less than their peers.

10% of the score
🔬
