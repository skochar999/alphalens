How We Pick Our Funds

📈

Did it beat its index?

We compare each fund to its own benchmark index. A fund that consistently beats its index is earning its fees.

40% of the score
🎯

How often did it win?

A fund that beats its index 7 out of 10 months is more trustworthy than one that had a single great year. Consistency matters.

35% of the score
🧭

Does it stick to its plan?

Fund managers who keep changing their approach are harder to trust. We reward funds that stay true to their stated strategy.

15% of the score
💸

Are the fees fair?

Annual fees eat into your returns every single year. We favour funds that charge less than their peers.

10% of the score
